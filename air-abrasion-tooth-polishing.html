What is Air Abrasion Tooth Polishing?

ir abrasion tooth polishing, or dental air polishing, is a modern air flow tooth cleaning method that uses a controlled jet of air, water, and a fine abrasive powder, typically sodium bicarbonate or glycine, to gently remove plaque, biofilm, and surface stains from your teeth. This technique targets stains caused by coffee, tea, red wine, smoking, and more.

Unlike traditional scaling, air polishing provides a deeper clean by reaching areas that are difficult to access with regular brushing or conventional methods. It’s an effective way to enhance your oral health and brighten your smile.
